# PART XV — Dealbreakers & Must-Haves
> These final questions are filters, not preferences. Answer "dealbreaker" or "must-have" only if you truly would refuse a place that fails the test. Each dealbreaker removes locations from consideration before scoring (see Step 6 of the Scoring Methodology). The last two questions set your top priorities, which double or increase the weight of certain scales.
