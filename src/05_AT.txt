# PART V — Identity: Assertive (A) vs. Turbulent (T)
> This optional fifth dimension explores emotional steadiness and self-assurance. Assertive types tend to be calm, confident, and resistant to stress; Turbulent types tend to be self-conscious, striving, and sensitive to stress, which often fuels high standards and growth. It is scored separately and does not change your four-letter type.
